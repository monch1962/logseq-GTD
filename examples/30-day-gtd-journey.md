### What Worked Well
1. **Starter Kit approach** - Beginning with 5 templates reduced overwhelm

4. **Customizable settings** - Able to adapt to personal workflow
5. **Integrated FAQ** - Answers available when needed

### Challenges Overcome
1. **Initial setup time** - Solved with quick install guide
2. **Template overload** - Solved with phased implementation
3. **Mobile performance** - Optimized through testing
4. **Context switching** - Improved with MRU dropdowns
5. **Review consistency** - Established with reminder system
