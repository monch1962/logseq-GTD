# Logseq GTD Templates - Complete Index

## 📋 Overview
This index provides a complete overview of all 15 GTD templates organized by GTD phase and complexity level. Use this index to discover templates, understand their purpose, and navigate to the right template for your needs.

**Purpose:** Collect everything that has your attention

| Template | Description | GTD Compliance | Difficulty | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------------|------------|----------|
